VERIQ Token Utility

The proposed applications and future use cases of the VRQ ecosystem. Features marked as planned are not represented as currently operational.

UTILITY 01

Business Verification

VERIQ aims to develop a business verification framework for eligible online stores and e-commerce businesses. The proposed VERIQ Verified Badge would help consumers identify businesses that have completed the applicable verification process. A future model may involve committing or staking VRQ tokens subject to published eligibility rules.

PLANNED
UTILITY 02

AI Media Authenticity

The project aims to research and develop AI-powered media analysis tools to examine potentially synthetic, manipulated or digitally altered images and other media.

IN DEVELOPMENT / PLANNED
UTILITY 03

Cryptographic Verification

Future verification services are intended to explore cryptographic methods for recording and checking digital authenticity evidence and verification records.

PLANNED

Technology & Research

The technical direction behind the proposed VERIQ ecosystem.

Blockchain Infrastructure

VERIQ uses an Ethereum-based ERC-20 token as the foundation of its proposed ecosystem. Public blockchain infrastructure provides transparent access to token contract information and transactions.

AI Media Analysis

VERIQ's research direction includes AI-based analysis of synthetic and manipulated media. The objective is to explore methods that can help assess digital media authenticity.

Quantum-AI Research Direction

The project also explores research directions involving quantum computing concepts and AI-based verification. These are research objectives and should not be interpreted as an operational quantum verification product.
