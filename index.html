Project overview · November 2026 · Public information draft

VERIQ is a project focused on improving trust in digital commerce and online information. Its long-term direction includes business verification, public verification profiles and research into AI-assisted detection of manipulated digital media. The project aims to make useful trust signals easier for people and platforms to inspect.

2. The problem

Consumers may struggle to assess unfamiliar online stores, while manipulated images and videos can make digital information harder to evaluate. Existing trust signals can be fragmented, difficult to verify or unavailable in a consistent public format.

3. Proposed ecosystem

Veriq Verified Badge: A proposed badge for eligible online businesses that complete a defined review process. The intended outcome is a public profile that communicates what was checked and when. A badge should not be interpreted as a guarantee of future conduct, product quality or transaction outcome.

Media authenticity research: VERIQ's QUANTIFICIAL research direction explores AI and advanced computational methods for detecting signs of image, video or media manipulation. Detection systems can make errors; results should be presented with limitations and appropriate confidence information.

Open tools: Longer-term plans include dApps, APIs and consumer-facing utilities for accessing verification information.

  • Symbol: VRQ
