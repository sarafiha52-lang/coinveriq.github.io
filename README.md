
# VERIQ — Digital Verification Ecosystem

**VERIQ (VRQ)** is an Ethereum-based project focused on exploring digital business verification, AI-generated media authenticity analysis, and cryptographic verification technologies.

The project's long-term objective is to support greater trust and transparency in digital environments.

## Project Overview

VERIQ is being developed around three areas:

- **Digital Business Verification:** A proposed verification framework intended to help users assess the credibility of eligible online businesses.
- **AI Media Authenticity:** Research and development focused on identifying manipulated and AI-generated media.
- **Cryptographic Verification:** Exploring cryptographic methods for recording and checking digital verification information.

These are development objectives. Features that have not been implemented are not presented as currently operational services.

## Planned Token Utility

The project is exploring the following potential uses for VRQ:

### 1. Veriq Verified Badge

A proposed system through which eligible online businesses may commit or stake VRQ tokens as part of a business verification framework.

### 2. AI Media Authenticity

Research into AI-assisted methods for analyzing digital images and identifying potentially manipulated or synthetic media.

### 3. Cryptographic Verification

Exploration of cryptographic verification structures to support the integrity and traceability of digital information.

**Development status:** These utilities are planned project objectives. Their availability and implementation will depend on future development.

## Technology Direction

VERIQ's technology direction includes research into:
